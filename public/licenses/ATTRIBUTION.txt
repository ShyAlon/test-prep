Expanded roster and visual modes (2026-11-24)

The roster now has three visual worlds: SRD creatures, mythological creatures,
and natural animals. Mode selection changes companions and collection views,
not questions or previously earned rewards. Folklore/animal labels in the latter
two worlds are not presented as licensed SRD rules.

Additional artwork sources (all obtained from the creator's itch.io download):

  https://pulsarcp.itch.io/free-pixel-art-animals-birds
  Used selected idle frames from the four animal/bird archives (24–96 px cells).

Modifications: selected idle frames cropped to consistent square canvases;
illustrations cropped and downsampled to at most 512px. Some original illustrated
backgrounds are retained. Sprite animations use their original idle frames;
static illustrations use gentle CSS motion, not invented frame animations.
Source files and original/output dimensions are listed in creature-art.json.
The file creature-roster.json lists all SRD names and source pages used, plus the
separate folklore/animal labels. The exact SRD attribution above still applies.
The existing independent CC0 notices remain applicable to retained earlier art.
